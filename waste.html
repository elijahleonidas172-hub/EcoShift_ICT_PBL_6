Waste Reduction for Cleaner Communities

Better Living Through the 4 Rs

Small Habits Create Cleaner Cities

Reducing waste helps protect streets, rivers, neighborhoods, and the environment from pollution. Every action matters.

4Rs

Refuse • Reduce • Reuse • Repair

Less Waste

Cleaner & Healthier Communities

Refuse & Reduce

Say no to single-use plastics at markets and shops to keep streets and waterways cleaner.

Reuse & Repair

Fix damaged items instead of throwing them away to reduce landfill waste and save energy.

Compost

Turn organic kitchen waste into fertilizer for gardens and reduce methane release from trash.
