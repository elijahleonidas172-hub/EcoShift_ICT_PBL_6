About Us

EcoShift is a digital platform created to help Myanmar citizens understand how simple daily choices can reduce carbon footprints. Our goal is to promote healthier lifestyles while addressing climate change through practical and easy actions that anyone can follow.

Our Mission

Inspiring Sustainable Habits For Everyday Life

EcoShift encourages students and communities to adopt environmentally friendly habits that reduce pollution, lower carbon emissions, and improve public health.

Promote climate awareness in Myanmar

Support healthier urban communities
