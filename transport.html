Low-Carbon Transport Options in Myanmar

Travel Smart for Cleaner Air

Cleaner Transportation Creates Healthier Cities

Reducing vehicle emissions helps improve air quality, lower fuel consumption, and build greener communities throughout Myanmar.

01

Walk or Cycle

For short trips in town, walking or biking produces zero emissions and improves cardiovascular health.

Zero Emissions
02

Share Your Ride

Use public buses or carpool with neighbors to reduce the number of vehicles on the road each day.

Fewer Cars, Cleaner Air
03

Plan Ahead

Combine multiple errands into one trip to save fuel, reduce emissions, and spend less time in traffic.

Smarter Fuel Usage
Why It Matters

Transportation Choices Affect Climate Change

Cars, motorcycles, and fuel-powered vehicles release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere. Choosing greener transport options can help lower pollution and protect the environment.

Reduces yearly emissions by 20–30%.
